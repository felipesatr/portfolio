# Final CTA and footer — hiring + freelance

Reviewed 8 October 2026. Research/proposals only: the homepage CTA, footer and reviews have not been changed.

Brief: reach recruiters/hiring managers and freelance clients after they have reviewed the work. Position Felipe as a Webapp Designer & Front-End Developer, with design-to-production contribution. Contact/email is primary; CV is secondary.

These are 20 useful first-party references, **not a ranking of measured conversion rates**. None of the inspected sites provides independently verified hiring-conversion data. Some are direct hiring references; others demonstrate freelance contact or restrained footer patterns. Recommendations below are my interpretation of those patterns.

## Best fit

Combine Sandeep's audience clarity, Rachel's design/engineering identity, Marco/Matt Ahrens's contact + CV pairing, and Arturo's human tone. Use Dennis's large closing invitation as a visual reference, not a claim about conversion performance.

## Original wording options

All six use **Email me** as the primary button and **View my CV** as the quieter secondary button. “Let's talk” can replace the primary label if it opens the Contact page instead of email.

1. **Need a designer who can build it, too?**
   I design and build web experiences, from the first ideas to the details that make them work. Have a role or freelance project in mind? Let's talk.

2. **Have a role or a project in mind?**
   I'd love to hear what you're working on—and where I could help with design, front-end development, or both.

3. **Let's build something worth using.**
   I'm interested in teams and freelance projects that care about thoughtful design and how it works in the browser.

4. **From the idea to the interface.**
   Looking for someone to join your team or help bring a web project to life? Let's start with a conversation.

5. **Good work starts with a conversation.**
   Tell me about your team, the role, or the project. I'll tell you where my design and front-end experience could fit.

6. **Your next web project, from design to build.**
   I work across interface design and front-end development. Let's talk about your next project—or the next person your team needs.

**My first choice is 1**: it communicates the distinctive design/build combination and welcomes both audiences. **2 is the clearest and least sales-like**. **3 has the most personality** without promising unverified outcomes.

## Footer layout options

- **A — Calm professional:** large CTA above; below, Felipe Salazar / Webapp Designer & Front-End Developer, visible email, LinkedIn, CV, then a compact legal line. My preferred direction.
- **B — Two audience routes:** same shared CTA; small “Hiring?” link to CV + Contact and “Have a project?” link to Contact. Helpful only if the routes actually differ; otherwise two headings add unnecessary choices.
- **C — Personal signature:** CTA and email, then a short “Designed and built by Felipe” line with location, LinkedIn and CV. Keeps the expressive page ending human.

Keep the actual email visible even if there is a button; a copy button is optional and should confirm “Copied.” Do not rely on mailto alone if a visitor has no mail app configured. Keep availability, location and remote/freelance statements accurate. No response-time promises, client claims or hiring statistics have been invented.

## Useful UX improvements before launch

Replace the CV and email placeholders with real destinations; this matters more than fine-tuning the final phrase. Make the contact page clearly accept both a role and a project without requiring a long form. A subject suggestion such as “Role opportunity” or “Project inquiry” can help without forcing two separate workflows. Retain keyboard focus and reduced-motion support for every closing action.
